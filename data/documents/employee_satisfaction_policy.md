# Employee Satisfaction Policy
**Document ID:** HR-005 | **Version:** 1.3 | **Owner:** Chief People Officer

## Purpose
Acme's ability to attract and retain talent depends on a workplace where employees feel heard, valued, and able to grow. This policy defines how satisfaction is measured, reported, and acted upon.

## Scope
Applies to all full-time employees and contractors engaged for more than 90 days. Survey data is anonymised; no individual's response is disclosed to managers.

## Satisfaction Measurement Instruments

### Quarterly Pulse Survey
A short (8-question) survey sent to all employees at the start of each quarter. Topics covered:
- Manager effectiveness and communication.
- Clarity of role and growth path.
- Workload and work-life balance.
- Confidence in company direction.
- Sense of belonging and inclusion.
- Likelihood to recommend Acme as a place to work (eNPS question).

Participation target: ≥ 80% response rate. Departments below 60% trigger a follow-up nudge from the CPO.

### Employee Net Promoter Score (eNPS)
eNPS is calculated quarterly from the question: "On a scale of 0–10, how likely are you to recommend Acme as a great place to work?"
- **Promoters:** 9–10
- **Passives:** 7–8
- **Detractors:** 0–6

eNPS = (% Promoters) − (% Detractors). Acme's target eNPS is ≥ +30.

### Annual Engagement Deep-Dive
A comprehensive 40-question survey run in Q4, administered by an independent third party to ensure anonymity. Results are benchmarked against industry peers. Department-level results (minimum 5 respondents) are shared with department heads.

## Reporting and Transparency
- Aggregate pulse-survey results are shared with all employees within two weeks of survey close.
- Department-level results are shared with each department head and their manager.
- The CPO presents eNPS trend analysis to the Executive Leadership Team quarterly.
- Individual responses are never disclosed; teams with fewer than five respondents have their results merged with a peer team before reporting.

## Manager Accountability
- Manager effectiveness scores from direct reports are included in manager performance reviews.
- Managers whose teams score below the 25th percentile for two consecutive quarters must complete a People Manager Coaching programme (6 sessions over 12 weeks).
- Managers who dismiss or fail to act on survey feedback within the action-planning window are flagged to their VP.

## Action Planning
Within four weeks of each pulse survey close:
1. Each department head reviews their team's results with their HR Business Partner.
2. A minimum of two action items are identified and logged in the HR system with owners and target dates.
3. Progress on prior-quarter action items is reviewed at the same meeting.
4. If a department's score has declined for two consecutive quarters, a root-cause workshop is facilitated by HR within six weeks.

## Employee Feedback Channels (Year-Round)
Employees are encouraged to raise concerns outside survey cycles through:
- **Direct manager 1:1s** – Weekly cadence recommended.
- **Skip-level meetings** – Available on request with any manager above the employee's direct manager.
- **HR confidential inbox** (hr-confidential@acme.example) – Reviewed by HR Business Partners only.
- **Anonymous suggestion portal** – Accessible via the intranet; submissions reviewed monthly by the CPO and a rotating panel of employees.

## Satisfaction Benchmarks and Escalation
| eNPS Range | Status | Action |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ +40 | Excellent | Celebrate; share practices across teams |
| +20 to +39 | Healthy | Continue current programmes |
| 0 to +19 | Watch | CPO-led review; targeted action plan within 30 days |
| < 0 | Critical | Executive escalation; external consultant engaged within 60 days |

## Confidentiality and Non-Retaliation
Survey data is used solely to improve the employee experience. Retaliation against any employee for survey participation or feedback is a violation of the Code of Conduct and may result in immediate disciplinary action.
