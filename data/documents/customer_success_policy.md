# Customer Success Policy
**Document ID:** CS-004 | **Version:** 2.0 | **Owner:** VP of Customer Success

## Purpose
Customer Success at Acme is measured by outcomes, not activity. This policy defines how Customer Success Managers (CSMs) engage customers throughout the lifecycle to maximise adoption, retention, and expansion.

## Customer Segmentation and Coverage Model
Customers are segmented by Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) and strategic importance:

| Tier | ARR Range | Coverage Model | Assigned Resource |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enterprise | > $100,000 | High-touch, dedicated | Named CSM + Solutions Architect |
| Mid-Market | $20,000 – $100,000 | Tech-touch + periodic human | Pooled CSM (1:40 ratio) |
| SMB | < $20,000 | Scaled digital (automated) | Self-service + community |

## Onboarding
All new customers receive an onboarding experience appropriate to their tier:

**Enterprise:**
- Kickoff call within 3 business days of contract signing.
- Dedicated Slack or Teams channel for the first 90 days.
- Week 1: environment configuration and admin training.
- Week 2–4: use-case mapping and integration setup.
- Day 30, 60, 90 health checks with documented success metrics.

**Mid-Market:**
- Welcome email and onboarding guide within 24 hours of signup.
- Group onboarding webinar within 5 business days.
- 30-day check-in call with CSM.

**SMB:**
- Automated onboarding email sequence over 14 days.
- In-app guided tours and a help-centre knowledgebase.
- Option to book a 30-minute onboarding call on-demand.

## Customer Health Scoring
Each account is assigned a Health Score (0–100) updated weekly from:
- **Product adoption** (30%): DAU/MAU ratio, features used vs. licensed.
- **Support signals** (20%): Open P1/P2 tickets, days since last escalation.
- **Engagement** (20%): Last CSM interaction, response rate to QBR invites, NPS/CSAT scores.
- **Commercial** (30%): Days to next renewal, expansion vs. contraction, payment status.

Health score thresholds:
- **Green (70–100):** Normal cadence.
- **Yellow (40–69):** CSM initiates outreach within 5 business days.
- **Red (0–39):** Churn-risk account; CSM escalates to VP of Customer Success within 48 hours; executive sponsor engaged if ARR > $50,000.

## Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs)
Enterprise customers receive a formal QBR every quarter:
- Agenda: ROI achieved, adoption metrics, roadmap preview, renewal timeline, expansion opportunities.
- CSM prepares a deck using the approved QBR template at least 3 business days before the meeting.
- Action items logged in CRM within 24 hours post-meeting.

Mid-Market customers receive a semi-annual business review.

## Renewal Process
- Renewal outreach begins 120 days before contract end for Enterprise accounts and 60 days for Mid-Market.
- CSM owns the renewal conversation; Sales is looped in if expansion is on the table.
- Renewal risk flagged in the CRM triggers a joint CSM-Sales churn-prevention call within 5 business days.

## Churn Prevention Playbook
When an account reaches Red health score or signals intent to cancel:
1. CSM schedules a discovery call within 48 hours to identify root cause.
2. If technical: Solutions Architect joins to create a remediation plan.
3. If value perception: CSM prepares an ROI report with quantified impact data.
4. If competitive: Sales and CSM co-present a comparison and retention offer.
5. If unresolvable: CSM documents lessons learned and initiates offboarding gracefully to protect brand reputation.
